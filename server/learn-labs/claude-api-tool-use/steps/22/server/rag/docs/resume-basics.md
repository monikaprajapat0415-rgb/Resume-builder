# Resume basics

A good resume is one or two pages long. It tells a recruiter in ten seconds who you are and what you can do. Start with your name, phone number, email and city at the top. Add a two-line summary that names your job title, your years of experience and your strongest skills.

List your work experience with the newest job first. For each job write the company, your title and the dates. Under it, write three to five short bullet points that start with an action verb, such as "Managed", "Built" or "Reduced". Where you can, add a number: how many customers you served, how much time you saved, how large your team was.

Keep education short: degree, institute and year. Freshers can put education above experience and add projects, internships and training courses. Leave out your photo, date of birth and marital status unless the employer asks for them.

Use one simple font such as Arial or Calibri at size 10 or 11. Save the file as a PDF unless the job post asks for Word.
