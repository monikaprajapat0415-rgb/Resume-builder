# Interview tips

Prepare before the interview by reading the job post again and writing down three stories from your past work. Each story should show a problem, what you did and the result. These stories answer most questions, including "Tell me about a time when...".

For the question "Tell me about yourself", talk for one minute: your current or last role, one or two achievements, and why this job is the next step. Do not read out your whole resume.

Arrive ten minutes early for an in-person interview and test your camera, microphone and internet an hour before a video interview. Dress one level above the daily dress at that workplace. Carry copies of your resume and any certificates.

When asked about salary, give a range based on the market for that role and city, and say it is open to discussion. At the end ask two questions of your own, for example about the first three months in the job or how success is measured. After the interview send a short thank-you message the same day.
