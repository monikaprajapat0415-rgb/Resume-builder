# Cover letters

A cover letter is a short note, around 200 to 300 words, that explains why you want this job at this company. It is not a copy of your resume. Use three short paragraphs.

In the first paragraph name the job you are applying for and one reason you are a strong fit. In the second paragraph give one or two examples from your work that match what the job needs, with a result if you have one. In the third paragraph thank the reader and say you would welcome an interview.

Address the letter to a named person when you can find the hiring manager on the company page. If not, write "Dear Hiring Team". Do not write "To whom it may concern".

Only send a cover letter when the job post asks for one or when you have something useful to add, such as a change of career or a gap you want to explain. Always check the company name and job title before you send, because a wrong name is the most common mistake.
