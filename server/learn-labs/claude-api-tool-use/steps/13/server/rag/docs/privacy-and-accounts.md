# Privacy and accounts (sample help-center text)

This folder holds sample help-center articles for the lab. They describe an imaginary resume website, not any real company's policy.

On the imaginary site, your name, email and phone number stay private until you accept a request from an employer. An employer first sees your skills and experience only. Contact details are shared after you accept.

You can download or delete your resume at any time from your account. When you delete the account, saved resumes and applications are removed. Some records, such as payment receipts, may be kept for the period the law requires.

When you use AI tools on a site like this, the text you submit is sent to an AI provider to produce the result. Never paste passwords, bank details or identity numbers into any AI tool.

If you forget your password, use the "Forgot password" link on the login page. A one-time link is sent to your registered email address.
