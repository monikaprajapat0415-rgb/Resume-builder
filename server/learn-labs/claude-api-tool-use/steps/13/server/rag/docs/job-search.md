# Job search in India

Start with a clear target: one job title, one or two cities and a salary range. Applying to everything makes your resume vague and your search tiring. Update your profile on job portals and on LinkedIn so that your title, skills and location match your resume.

Apply early. Many employers review the first fifty to one hundred applications first, so applying within two days of a job being posted raises your chances. Set up job alerts for your title and city so you hear about new posts quickly.

Use your network. Tell former colleagues, friends and teachers that you are looking, and ask for referrals. A referral often gets your resume read by a person instead of a filter.

For factory, driver, security and daily wage roles, walk-in drives and local contractors are common. Carry your identity proof, photographs and any trade certificate, and keep a simple one-page resume with your phone number at the top. Beware of any job that asks for money before hiring: real employers do not charge job seekers.
