Info.plist keys and capabilities used by projects 8 to 10
(In Xcode: select the project, pick the app target, then Info and Signing & Capabilities.)

Project 8, Fitness (StepTracker)
  Capability:   HealthKit
  Info.plist:   Privacy - Health Share Usage Description
                "Steps are read to show your daily progress. They never leave your phone."

Project 9, Music (MusicPlayer)
  Capability:   Background Modes, tick "Audio, AirPlay, and Picture in Picture"

Project 10, QR scanner (QRScanner)
  Info.plist:   Privacy - Camera Usage Description
                "The camera is used to scan QR codes. Pictures are not saved or sent anywhere."
  Needs a real iPhone (the Simulator has no camera).

The other projects need no special capability.
